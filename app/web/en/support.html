Common questions

Why can't I paste?
Saenggak is a place only for writing people typed themselves, so posts have to be typed by hand.

Can I pick a profile photo from my gallery?
No. Only a photo of your own face, taken with the camera, can be used.

I can't follow someone.
Following opens once both of you have written on 10 days.

How do I delete my account?
Go to Profile > Settings > Delete account in the app. Your posts and records are all deleted.
