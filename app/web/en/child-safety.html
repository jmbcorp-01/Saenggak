Child Safety Standards

Effective November 27, 2026

Saenggak (the "Service") has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM) in any form. JMB Corporation Co., Ltd. (the "Company") runs the Service under the standards below.

1. What is prohibited

  • Posting, sharing or requesting child sexual abuse material (CSAM)
  • Text or images that sexualize minors
  • Grooming, sexual exploitation, sextortion or trafficking of minors
  • Helping, promoting or glorifying any of the above

2. How to report

Use the in-app Report option on any post or profile. If you cannot use the app, email support@saenggak.app.

3. What we do

  • Child safety reports are reviewed ahead of all other reports.
  • Violating content is removed immediately and the account is permanently banned.
  • We report CSAM to law enforcement and the relevant authorities under the laws of the Republic of Korea, and to regional bodies such as the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) where applicable.
  • We preserve and provide data to investigators as the law requires.

4. Age requirement

You must be 14 or older to use the Service.

5. Child safety contact

Questions about this policy or how reports are handled: support@saenggak.app
