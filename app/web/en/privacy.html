Privacy Policy

Effective December 1, 2026 · JMB Corporation Co., Ltd.

This is a translation for convenience. If it differs from the Korean version, the Korean version governs.

JMB Corporation Co., Ltd. (the "Company") runs the Saenggak service on the web and in the iOS and Android apps (the "Service"), and protects users' personal information in line with the Korean Personal Information Protection Act and related laws. This policy explains what personal information we process and why, how long we keep it, and your rights.

For each post we automatically work out a "thought score" from the typing record and the text, and use it only to order the 추천 (For you) tab. That order also reflects posts you related to. The score is never shown, and it is never the sole reason a post is removed or an account limited.

2. How long we keep it

We destroy your personal information without delay when you delete your account (Settings > Delete account in the app). Your posts, relates, follows, profile photo and sign-in account are deleted immediately and cannot be recovered. The following is kept for a set period, as required by law or to prevent abuse, and then destroyed:

  • Access logs: 3 months (Protection of Communications Secrets Act)
  • Moderator actions (suspensions, post removals and so on) and reports: 1 year after handling (for disputes)

Electronic files are deleted in a way that cannot be recovered.

If you do not want your information transferred, you can refuse by deleting your account, but you will then be unable to use the Service.

4. Sharing with third parties

We do not give your personal information to third parties, except when law enforcement or other authorities request it through lawful procedures. The posts, name, username and profile photo you make public in the Service are visible to other users.

5. Your rights

You can access, correct, delete or stop the processing of your personal information at any time. You can edit your profile in the app and delete your account in Settings > Delete account. In Settings > Export my data you can download what we store about you. For any other request, email the privacy officer below and we will handle it within 10 days.

6. Cookies and similar technology

The Service does not use cookies for advertising or tracking. Only the information needed to keep you signed in is kept in your device's secure storage (iOS Keychain, Android encrypted storage or your browser's storage), and it is removed when you sign out.

7. Security

All communication is encrypted with HTTPS. The database and photo storage are encrypted and cannot be reached directly from outside. Passwords are stored in a form the Company cannot read, moderator access is given to as few people as possible, and every moderator action is logged.

If this policy changes, we will announce it in the Service 7 days before the change takes effect (30 days for changes that are unfavorable to users).
